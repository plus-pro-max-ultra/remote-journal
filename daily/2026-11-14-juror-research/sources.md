https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5fbe9ee0e90e077ed7351b0a/j001-eng.pdf

## 3. Juror who researched law on child neglect gets suspended sentence（Charlie Moloney, Law Gazette，2024-05-10）

https://www.lawgazette.co.uk/news/juror-who-researched-law-on-child-neglect-gets-suspended-sentence/5119662.article

## 4. Contempt of court: juror misconduct and internet publications（Law Commission）

https://lawcom.gov.uk/project/contempt-of-court-juror-misconduct-and-internet-publications/

## 5. The 21st Century Jury: Contempt, Bias and the Impact of Jury Service（Cheryl Thomas，Criminal Law Review，2020）

https://www.ucl.ac.uk/laws/sites/laws/files/21st_century_jury_clr_article_final.pdf

第五節：2017 Old Bailey前後階段及2018–2019其他法院調查、書面通知設計與規則辨識改善；非隨機個人試驗，問卷理解不是實際違規率或裁決正確率。
